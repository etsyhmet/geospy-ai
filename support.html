How do I analyze a photo?

Open GeoSpy AI, pick a photo from your library or take a new one, then tap “Analyze Location.” The AI returns the most likely place along with a confidence score.

Why is a result not exact?

When a photo has no embedded GPS data, the location is predicted purely from visual cues like landmarks, architecture, and terrain, so it is an estimate. Photos with EXIF GPS data give an exact fix.

What does the subscription include?

Premium unlocks full location details, the interactive map, and unlimited analyses. It is offered as an auto-renewable weekly or yearly subscription.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

Go to the Settings app on your device → tap your name → Subscriptions → GeoSpy AI, where you can change or cancel at any time. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period.
